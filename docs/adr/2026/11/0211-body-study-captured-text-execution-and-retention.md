# ADR 0211: Body-study captured-text execution and report retention

## Status

Accepted implementation correction under the selected ADR 0206 protocol.
The successor requires scoped proof and independent review before execution.

## Context

The fourth BODY attempt exposed quality observations, but a metadata-only
applicability guard returned unavailable for ready captured-text v2 slots
before PostgreSQL lookup. Fusion then lacked one of its required inputs. These
states are technical incompleteness. A well-formed semantic refusal under the
registered criterion remains a distinct observation. The attempt and its
exposed records remain in the original journal and are ineligible as a
primary result.

The complete report also exceeded the historical BODY host reader limit.
The journal commits the full report and any final failure envelope using
different fields. Both commitments are required when both artifacts exist.

## Decision

Allow the registered BODY-only captured-text v2 path to use the existing exact
lookup when the ready slot binds the same task, PostgreSQL publication and
captured-text representation. Require ranked discovery and an existing
ranked-positive or absent-answer rule. Check live declared body policy for all
selected bindings before accessing vectors. Preserve the metadata applicability
rule for metadata and v1, the existing private and unsupported dispositions,
the paired FTS requirement, the fusion algorithm and the grading protocol.

Give the full report and the two failure artifacts a shared 32 MiB budget.
A full report alone may use the entire budget. Each failure artifact has a
4 MiB member limit. The journal has at most 900 events of 16 KiB, and sealed originals
have a 16 MiB aggregate cap. These categories total 62.0625 MiB.
The canonical exporter enforces the 64 MiB content ceiling on the whole
export, including the remaining metadata, and retains its 72 MiB archive
ceiling. BODY readers and writers enforce the member caps. Keep the original
preparation reports once inside the retained matrix to reserve room for the
additional observations. Preserve all sealed originals, slots and grades.

Join the complete report to the terminal's report identity and common set.
Join the final failure envelope to the separate failure identity and sealed
observation census. Require any pre-cleanup envelope to match the final
failure's pre-cleanup commitment. Keep partial evidence available when any
required artifact or identity disagrees. Retention of an incomplete result establishes artifact
custody only. Account for the archive, bounded readbacks and EOF probes within
the unchanged 12 GiB controlled-read cap.

## Verification and boundaries

Prove the precise captured-text admission population and refusal paths, both
BODY readers above the old size guard, combined complete and incomplete
retention, and conflicting or missing terminal identities. With the current
source and byte forecast independently reviewed, the first complete admissible
successor is primary even if its result is negative or below the common floor.
The registered cohort, controls, scoring, held-out access and production
admission remain governed by ADR 0206.
