# Cloe artwork

Cloe is the Common Lore Index mascot, a 27-year-old data scientist who studies
knowledge graphs. The research-studio illustration adapts the original
Muse avatar concept in the Knowledge Forge AI house style.

The full-frame SVG study preserves the composition and the 1024 × 1536
artboard. The site serves its Standard 94 and responsive Fine 96 Sharp WebP
exports. The original illustration remains available in the
[organization gallery](https://www.knowledge-forge.ai/gallery/).

The mascot art is reserved separately from the repository's software license.
Publication on the project website and organization gallery does not grant
third-party reuse rights.
